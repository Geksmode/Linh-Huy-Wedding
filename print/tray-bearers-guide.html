Lễ Ăn Hỏi · Linh & Huy

Tray Bearers Guide

Engagement ceremony at the bride's family home  ·  Main ceremony at 15:00

Your call times

Bride's side · female bearers

Arrive at Linh's home at 14:00
  • Matching áo dài on, hair and outfit checked.
  • You receive the trays from the groom's team and carry them inside.

Groom's side · male bearers

Gather at the hotel at 14:00
  • Matching outfit on, then move with the groom's procession (14:00 – 14:10).
  • Walk in with the procession at 14:45, tray in hand.
  • You carry the trays in, hand them over to the bride's team, then help bring them inside.

Good to know

Do

  • Be on time: the whole schedule hangs on the 15:00 ceremony.
  • Carry trays with both hands, at chest height, and keep them level.
  • Smile and enjoy it: the crew is capturing natural moments.

Avoid

  • Standing between the camera and the families during the ceremony.
  • Leaving before the group photo with the couple.
  • Using your phone during the handover and the ceremony.
